Reel labels · one UID per reel

Every reel gets its own identity (RL + ULID). The QR holds the whole reel, so a scan on the floor or on the job opens this exact reel, cuts and pulls included.

Layer labels and head tags

One layer label per cut across the reel, printed in winding order, goes on the shrink wrap over that cut (4 x 2, PD45 or ZQ630). One head tag per pulling head (2 x 0.75, ZD421). Each scan opens the reel on that exact pull.
